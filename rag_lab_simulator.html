Why RAG exists.

A

LLM aloneAnswers from learned parameters; may not know private, current, or niche information.

B

RAG retrievesSearches your knowledge base for passages relevant to the question.

C

Context groundsRelevant passages are supplied to the model as evidence for its answer.

D

EvaluateCheck whether the right evidence was found and the answer faithfully used it.

Key distinction

RAG usually does not retrain the LLM. It retrieves external information at answer time and places it in the prompt.

Glossary

Chunk — a small retrievable segment of a document.

Embedding — a numeric representation that can encode meaning.

Vector store — an index for finding similar embeddings.

Top-K — the K highest-ranked retrieved chunks.

Grounding — how well an answer is supported by its evidence.
